Co-operative Bank of Kenya — NPL Trajectory Reconciliation

A standalone record of sources, method, and what was and wasn't resolved, kept separate from the published piece itself.

Sources

  • Co-op Bank's quarterly Investor Briefings (Q3 2024 – Q1 2026)
  • Quarterly regulatory financial disclosure tables
  • FY2025 KPMG-audited Integrated Report, Note 4 (Financial Risk Management)

No press coverage, third-party summaries, or aggregator figures were used as a source for any number in the piece.

Method

  • Primary-source preference — every figure traced to a Bank-published document, never a recap
  • Cross-source triangulation — each headline Kshs figure checked against at least two independent Bank documents
  • Independent recomputation — every ratio rebuilt from source numbers, not taken as reported
  • Second audit pass — every number re-verified in a separate pass, reconciled line-by-line to reported closing balances, before publication

What was found

Group gross NPLs fell from Kshs 78.93Bn (Q3 2025 peak) to Kshs 71.37Bn (Q1 2026) — a 9.58% decline in the absolute balance, not just a ratio diluted by loan book growth.

Write-offs held a stable ~19.4% share of total NPL exits in both FY2024 and FY2025 — no spike in the year the ratio improved most, indicating the recovery came from cures and settlements, not an accounting cleanup.

What was checked and explained, not just flagged

A Kshs 1.14Bn gap between the audited Stage 3 balance and the regulatory NPL figure for the same date was traced to a structural difference between IFRS 9's qualitative classification standard and CBK's stricter arrears-only standard — not a reporting inconsistency.

Corroboration used, not relied on

Cytonn Investments and Mwango Capital had independently published figures broadly consistent with the Bank's own numbers. These were used only as corroboration after the primary reconciliation was complete — never as a source for any figure in the piece.

Disclosure

The Bank's Investor Relations office was notified directly, with the full analysis, before any public circulation — as a courtesy notice, not a request for comment.
