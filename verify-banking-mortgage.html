How we verified this

Mortgage Credit Risk Disclosure — Five Kenyan Lenders

A standalone record of sources, method, and what was and wasn't resolved, kept separate from the published piece itself.

Sources

  • Each institution's own primary financial disclosures — KCB, Equity, Stanbic, HFCB, and KMRC

No press coverage or third-party aggregation was used as a source for any figure in the piece.

Method

  • Primary-source preference — every figure traced to each institution's own published disclosures
  • Cross-source triangulation — KMRC funding relationships checked against each bank's own reporting, not KMRC's alone
  • Independent recomputation — year-on-year changes rebuilt from source figures rather than taken as reported
  • Six separate audit passes, each re-verifying the prior pass before publication

What was found

Three of the four banks profiled report only a blended, group-wide NPL ratio — with no way to isolate mortgage-specific credit risk from the public disclosures alone.

KMRC funding relationships were traced across three of the four banks, including a previously unsurfaced 179% year-on-year increase in one bank's KMRC balance.
