About

I independently reconcile institution-reported financial figures against regulator and primary-source data — closing information gaps the public can't otherwise see.

I started with SACCOs: a data-rich, under-scrutinized sector where I could build real rigor before I had institutional access. I'm working toward banking and fintech, where the same discipline carries more weight. Based in Nairobi, currently studying Economics and Data Analytics at Mount Kenya University.

Method

  1. Source

    Start only from what's already public — regulatory filings, audited disclosures, government reports. No leaked or unverifiable data.

  2. Reconcile

    Cross-reference every figure against the institution's own other disclosures, and against independent regulatory data where it exists.

  3. Recompute

    Rebuild the arithmetic from source numbers rather than trusting a summary figure. If it doesn't reconcile, that's the finding.

  4. Disclose

    State plainly what matched, what didn't, and what couldn't be verified at all. An unresolved figure is reported as unresolved, not rounded to a clean story.
